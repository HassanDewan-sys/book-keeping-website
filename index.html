How We Publish Your Book

A clear, collaborative path that turns your manuscript into a published,
best-selling book, with you in control at every step.

Step 01

Free Consultation & Manuscript Review

Before anything else, we talk. Send us your manuscript, share your goals, and we'll map exactly what your book needs, services, timeline and cost.

Step 02

Professional Editing

Great editing is invisible, readers just can't put the book down. Our editors work at every level, from structural development through final proofreading.

Step 03

Cover Design

Readers absolutely judge a book by its cover. Our designers craft covers that stop the scroll, with multiple concepts and unlimited revisions.

Step 04

Formatting

Your book has to look right everywhere, Kindle, Apple Books, print editions, all of it, formatted for every platform and device.

Print, ISBN & Distribution

Launch & Marketing

Publishing a book is one thing; getting it read is another. Amazon optimization, social campaigns, paid ads and author-platform building.
